# Preview index

These PNGs render the final self-contained GLBs with ordinary Three.js materials. The canvas is Reed's #121110. No preview tint is baked into the assets.

- `family-contact-sheet.png`: male and female rows; front, oblique, side, back oblique, back columns.
- `exercise-contact-sheet.png`: pectorals with deltoid/triceps secondary highlights, back groups, and quadriceps.
- `shapes-contact-sheet.png`: the same rows; default, adiposity=1, muscularity=1, both=1 columns. Combined views also set fullness_corrective=1.
- `highlights-contact-sheet.png`: pain front/back and muscle front/back views. Red indicates left shoulder, lower back, left knee and right ankle; blue quadriceps is primary, warm glutes/hamstrings are secondary.
- `<variant>-front/back/side/oblique.png`: 560 × 700 neutral-material views.
- `<variant>-adiposity/muscularity/combined-front/back/side.png`: endpoint shapes.
- `<variant>-combined-highlights.png`: morphed pain and muscle patches together.
- `<variant>-phone-front.png` and `-phone-highlights.png`: 288 × 360 images, approximately 296 pixels of body height.

Anatomical left appears on the viewer's right in front views. The figurines face glTF +Z. Lighting uses a soft hemisphere and two directional lights, with no floor, cinematic background, or shadow map. Colors, strength, view rotation and light setup are viewer choices.
