Direction C

Companion: someone's in the room with you

Warmer and softer. A warm charcoal instead of pure black, a friendly rounded sans, big soft shapes. The mascot is the hero of the home screen, lit by a faint glow in Reed blue, which is the one piece of atmosphere in the whole app. During a session it steps down to the corner and talks in bubbles.

This is the most emotionally inviting option and the easiest one to like on day one. The risk is drifting toward "wellness app", so the type and copy have to stay adult.
