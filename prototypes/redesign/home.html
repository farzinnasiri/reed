Reed · Home · Companion direction

One screen: the Pulse, the thread, the dock

The Pulse (top) is a glanceable strip showing your week, bodyweight and your nearest goal. Tap it and it grows into today's Progress view. During a session it becomes a live activity. The dock (bottom) never changes: a session button and the composer.

The middle has two modes. When you open the app you see today: Reed as the hero, the decision, and whatever it's bringing you (plan, weigh-in, quick log, summary). No old chats. The moment you talk, Reed glides into the bottom-left corner and the screen becomes one endless chat. Replies have no avatars; Reed is the presence in the corner, thinking or speaking. Scroll up and older conversations load in.

Frame 01 is live: tap a chip or the composer to watch the transition. The other frames are interactive too: tap the Pulse, "F", the weight steppers.
